Where The Wind Blows

Jace Russel Simon

To fly a kite, the wind shall blow,

To free the minds of those below,

To see a sorrow, sorrow throw,

To pick apart the wind a-blow,

We search our minds for ways to fly,

A kite perhaps, but not on by,

The kite it flies among the valley,

Below where the wind blows.

In Flanders Fields

John McCrae

In Flanders fields the poppies blow
Between the crosses, row on row,
That mark our place; and in the sky
The larks, still bravely singing, fly
Scarce heard amid the guns below.

We are the Dead. Short days ago
We lived, felt dawn, saw sunset glow,
Loved and were loved, and now we lie,
In Flanders fields.

Take up our quarrel with the foe:
To you from failing hands we throw
The torch; be yours to hold it high.
If ye break faith with us who die
We shall not sleep, though poppies grow
In Flanders fields.

Where The Valley Lies

Bianca Simon

through the clouds and the trees

a tree is planted

underneath the clouds and through the trees

love is buried knee deep

to which this love is burrowed plain

to where the valley lies
